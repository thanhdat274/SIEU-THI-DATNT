2. LEGAL RESEARCH — MANDATORY
==================================================

Research Vietnamese legislation effective as of
September 30, 2026.

Prioritize official sources:
- vanban.chinhphu.vn
- congbao.chinhphu.vn
- xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn
- chinhphu.vn
- mof.gov.vn
- gdt.gov.vn
- thuvienphapluat.vn for secondary reference

Research the following:

A. Business registration:
- Household businesses.
- Private enterprises.
- Single-member limited liability companies.
- Multi-member limited liability companies.
- Joint-stock companies.
- Conversion from household business to enterprise.
- Business locations and branches.

B. Taxation:
- Value Added Tax.
- Personal Income Tax.
- Corporate Income Tax.
- Special Consumption Tax when applicable.
- Import/export duties when applicable.
- Tax exemptions and reductions.
- Tax declaration and payment.
- Electronic invoices.
- Accounting obligations.
- Social insurance and payroll obligations.

C. Food-related business requirements:
- Food safety.
- Product origin.
- Food processing.
- Food and beverage retail.
- Relevant licensing conditions.

Research applicable laws and amendments,
including but not limited to:

- Law on Enterprises.
- Law on Tax Administration 108/2025/QH15.
- VAT Law 48/2024/QH15 and amendments.
- PIT Law 109/2025/QH15 and amendments.
- CIT Law 67/2025/QH15 and amendments.
- Resolution 198/2025/QH15.
- Decree 68/2026/ND-CP.
- Decree 141/2026/ND-CP.
- Decree 320/2025/ND-CP.
- Decree 254/2026/ND-CP.
- Applicable Ministry of Finance circulars.

IMPORTANT:
Verify each document's legal status, effective date,
amendments, transitional provisions, and applicability.

Do not use outdated tax thresholds.

Do not treat draft legislation as enacted law.

Do not assume the latest publication date means
the latest applicable legal rule.

If legislation cannot be verified, flag it as
UNVERIFIED and do not activate that rule.

- Single-member LLC.
- Multi-member LLC.
- Joint-stock company.

STAGE 5:

==================================================
4. HOUSEHOLD BUSINESS TAX ENGINE
==================================================

Implement:
- Annual revenue tracking.
- Taxable revenue classification.
- VAT calculations.
- PIT calculations.
- Revenue thresholds.
- Tax exemptions.
- Filing obligations.
- Electronic invoice obligations.
- Tax payment schedules.

Apply the verified 2026 legislation.

Explicitly verify the threshold introduced by
Decree 141/2026/ND-CP.

The current verified reference threshold is
VND 1,000,000,000 annual revenue.

Do not hardcode the older VND 500,000,000 threshold
as the current rule.

Support historical tax rules separately.

Classify business activities:
- Retail goods.
- Food preparation.
- Beverage services.
- Other taxable activities.

Do not assume every activity has the same VAT
or PIT calculation method.

Support eligible PIT calculation methods,
including revenue-based and income-based methods
where legally applicable.

==================================================
5. CORPORATE TAX ENGINE
==================================================

Implement:
- Corporate taxable revenue.
- Deductible expenses.
- Non-deductible expenses.
- Taxable income.
- Corporate Income Tax.
- VAT input/output.
- Tax incentives.
- Tax losses and carryforward rules.
- Tax payment and settlement.

Verify applicable CIT rates:

15%:
Eligible businesses with annual revenue
not exceeding VND 3 billion.

17%:
Eligible businesses with annual revenue
above VND 3 billion and not exceeding
VND 50 billion.

20%:
Standard rate, subject to statutory exceptions.

Verify revenue reference periods and exclusions.

Implement eligible exemptions under:
- Decree 141/2026/ND-CP.
- Resolution 198/2025/QH15.
- Relevant implementing legislation.

Never automatically grant tax exemptions
without verifying eligibility.

10. CHATGPT TAX ASSISTANT
==================================================

Create an in-game Vietnamese tax assistant.

Example questions:

"Tôi bán được 1,2 tỷ một năm thì đóng thuế gì?"

"Hộ kinh doanh và công ty TNHH khác nhau thế nào?"

"Thuế GTGT tính theo doanh thu hay lợi nhuận?"

"Khi nào phải xuất hóa đơn điện tử?"

"Thuê nhân viên có phải đóng bảo hiểm không?"

The assistant must:

- Retrieve current official legal sources.
- Explain applicable legal rules.
- Cite legal document numbers and provisions.
- Display effective dates.
- Distinguish historical and current legislation.
- Explain calculations using game data.
- Identify missing information.
- Avoid unsupported legal conclusions.

IMPORTANT:
ChatGPT must NOT be the tax calculation engine.

All tax amounts must be calculated by
the deterministic Tax Engine.

AI is responsible for explanations,
retrieval, and educational guidance.

Implement the assistant through a secure backend.

Never expose an OpenAI API key in the frontend.

Provide a fallback knowledge-base interface
when AI service is unavailable.

Do not assume ChatGPT subscription access
provides free API usage.
